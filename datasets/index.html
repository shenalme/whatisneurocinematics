Open film-viewing datasets

Datasets that record brain and body signals while people watch films or videos. Useful for neurocinematics, emotion recognition and computing inter-subject correlation.

Updated 12 October 2026

DatasetSignalsWhat's inside
DEAPEEG, peripheral physiology, face video32 participants watching 40 one-minute music video excerpts, with arousal, valence, liking and dominance ratings.
MAHNOB-HCIEEG, eye gaze, physiology, video, audio27 participants watching emotional film clips, with self-reported emotion and implicit tagging tasks.
AMIGOSEEG, ECG, GSR, video40 participants watching short and long videos, alone and in groups, with emotion and personality measures.
DREAMERPortable EEG, ECG23 participants watching 18 film clips, recorded with low-cost wireless devices, with arousal, valence and dominance ratings.
SEED62-channel EEG, eye movementsParticipants watching film clips chosen to evoke positive, neutral and negative emotions, over repeated sessions.
studyforrestfMRI (3T and 7T), eye tracking, physiologyParticipants watching and listening to the film Forrest Gump, with detailed annotations of the film's content.
Naturalistic Neuroimaging DatabasefMRI86 participants, each watching a full-length feature film in the scanner.
Healthy Brain NetworkEEG, eye trackingA large sample of children and adolescents, including recordings while watching short films such as a clip from Despicable Me.

Most datasets require you to accept a licence or end-user agreement before downloading. Check each provider's terms and cite the original paper when you use the data.

Choosing a dataset

  • For emotion recognition: DEAP, DREAMER, AMIGOS and SEED pair signals with emotion ratings.
  • For whole-film viewing: the Naturalistic Neuroimaging Database and studyforrest use full-length features rather than short clips.
  • For eye movements and attention: MAHNOB-HCI, studyforrest and the Healthy Brain Network include eye tracking.
  • For inter-subject correlation: any dataset where many people watched the same stimulus works; see the ISC explainer.
Know a dataset that belongs here? This list is maintained by a researcher in the field and is updated as new resources appear.
